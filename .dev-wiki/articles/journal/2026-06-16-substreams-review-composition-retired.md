## The completeness critic's reframe (the key insight)

"Retire composition-lift vs bet on P14 case-construction" is a **false binary**. The dominant option:
**declare detection-lift measured-and-closed — the triple-null is a finished, SHIPPED pitch asset**
("we publish our nulls"), not a problem to fix. Reaching for P14 (construct cases so no single
detector catches them) would ENGINEER composition-required by construction — a tautology that violates
the founding "typologies emerge, never injected" doctrine and would RETROACTIVELY POISON the honesty
that is the program's strongest selling point (negative expected value). Composition's value-prop
**splits**: detection-lift is measured-impossible on emergent synthetic data, but **evidence-assembly**
(the dossier), **redundancy-management** (the §13 fm-1 frontier, demonstrable TODAY on the committed
corpus's un-deduplicated 2,251 indicators × 5 regulators), and **volume-inversion** (N alerts → 1
dossier, a workload claim the triple-null says nothing about) all survive untouched. **The composition
frontier moves OFF the substrate (its job is done) onto the corpus and casework.**
