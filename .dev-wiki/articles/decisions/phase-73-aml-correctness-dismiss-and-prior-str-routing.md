## Context

The design fan-out's adversarial critics (CRITIQUE-1/2) caught two AML-realism errors in the first-draft
case: (a) the dismissal cleared the funnel alert on absence of a negative hit ("shares no identifier
with a known-bad") — AML-wrong, because an SME clears a funnel alert when the activity is affirmatively
EXPLAINED, not when a screen comes back empty; (b) the prior-human-trafficking-STR linkage was placed
on the OUTBOUND controller (money goes there) — backwards for ML-A5 corroboration (the predicate comes
from a known-bad among the funds' SOURCES, money came FROM there) — and stacked three coincidences
(controller + director + STR-link) on one synthetic identity.

## Decision

Accept both corrections as recommended:

1. **The DISMISS (Lakeshore) leads with AFFIRMATIVE business reconciliation + an ESTABLISHED source of
   funds** (inbound reconciles to the catering business, the forward-out is a recurring identifiable
   supplier, source established, volume fits the anticipated profile) — the clean caution-list/ER
   non-match is a CORROBORATOR, not the cause. The source-of-funds axis (Northgate unestablished /
   Lakeshore established) is the cleanest contrast and mirrors the live exemplar's C14. The dismissal is
   documented-by-substance, never "defensive filing".

2. **The prior-trafficking-STR hit sits on an INBOUND SOURCE party** (Vesna Maric, resolved by email to
   the prior_str_register) — "a SOURCE of the funnel's funds is linked to a prior-trafficking subject"
   is then literally true (ML-A5 on the inbound source). **The outbound controller** (James Calder, the
   subject's own director, who receives the structured sub-limit EMT drain) **is a DIFFERENT person** —
   the evidence is spread across two synthetic parties as a real network would be, de-concentrating the
   identity.

## Consequences

- Northgate's determination is licensed by INDEPENDENT legs (mechanism C2/C3 + ML-A7 source-not-
  established + ML-A4 network + ML-A5 inbound-source corroboration + named predicate read from the
  register), not by one over-loaded identity.
- The structured sub-limit EMT drain to the director makes the email-resolve honest to the rail that
  carried it (EMT's genuine routing key is email); structuring strengthens, not weakens, the funnel
  narrative.
- The throughput-vs-profile read REINFORCES the pass-through mechanism (ML-A1/A6); it does NOT count
  toward the ≥2-leg bar — an independent-legs honesty guard.
- The prior_str_register entry is itself authored synthetic; honesty_notes says exactly that (not "never
  authored"), and the prior-STR panel carries its own inline synthetic marker so a cropped screenshot
  self-identifies.
