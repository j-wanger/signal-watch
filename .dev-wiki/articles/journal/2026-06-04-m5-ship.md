## Decisions

- **M4 (live/pre-gen) skipped.** Pre-gen needs `fetch()` (breaks `file://`), so it is inert in the
  single-file ship artifact — always falls back to inline config. Scripted IS the ship path; the
  machinery would not change what the stage artifact does. (Subtraction test.)
- **M5 = doc/verify only, zero engine edits.** The real work was truing-up two stale + fentanyl-only
  docs to post-M3 / per-typology reality, then the compliance/offline gate.
- **Deferred:** closing "ask" slide (new act → touches the six-act-arc non-negotiable + needs
  config/schema; a content effort, not a ship task), product rename (kept "Signal Engine"),
  Playwright (a dep against the dependency-light agreement for a one-shot demo).
- **Ship target = single self-contained `dist/<id>/index.html` per typology** — resolves the
  hosted-vs-single [OPEN] to single-file (decision-log §10).
