session AFTER Phase 45 closed. **ESCAPE HATCH: USER OVERRIDE** — no open phase/tasks; the
user explicitly chose oneshot over a dedicated phase ("do we need a dedicated phase? or can

## What it is

32.8s seamless three-act loop (dossier-cinematic: grain, scanline, amber glow): ACT 1 a
FINTRAC operational-alert doc card streams in, six indicator highlights sweep and extract as
chips (the committed `red_flag` translations with real `md L<line>` provenance; rail header
carries the honest "68 derived from this alert"); ~3s deliberate HOLD on the finished page;
ACT 2 twelve hex atoms pop into a constellation (ten named from the record's REAL committed
signal names — S-INCOME-ACTIVITY, S-SHELL-NOMINEE, S-VASP-EXPOSURE, S-SHIPPING-PARCEL… — plus
two library atoms); ACT 3 the lattice re-wires through four escalating combinations (3→3→4→5
atoms) while a coverage arc ratchets 24→41→63→86%, labeled "coverage index · illustrative";
finale "Alone it's noise. Together it's a case." → reset. Persistent: brand lockup, act rail,
"Illustrative · vision prototype" chip, value-prop ticker.

  the user rules the booth non-commercial.
