## Context

The design fan-out's critique surfaced that the phase is secretly ~2.5 phases: T1 (the data authoring)
and the template/render are BOTH L, and a LITE phase tolerates only one L. The recommendation was to
descope — wire only the money-flow graph this phase, defer the entity-resolution + named-BO graphs and
the cross-pillar contract to a follow-on, and stay LITE. The project default is LITE (the Phase-67..72
companion-phase rhythm).

## Decision

The user OVERRODE the descope: **STANDARD ceremony for this phase — accept two L tasks and wire all
three graphs (money-flow / entity-resolution / named-BO) now.** STANDARD brings a formal spec before
task finalization, the full 7-category post-implementation self-check, and the unified reviewer
dispatch covering the sacred-engine change (the new affirmative-clear verdict). Alternatives rejected:
LITE with the money-flow-only descope (under-delivers the network depth that IS the thesis — "the
network is the difference"); two LITE phases (splits the matched pair's payoff across sessions).

## Consequences

- Two L tasks (T1 data authoring, T4 render) — explicitly permitted under STANDARD; the reviewer
  dispatch is the quality gate for the engine touch.
- A spec precedes task finalization; the post-implementation self-check runs all 7 categories.
- The cross-pillar contract doc stays deferred (A6) regardless of ceremony — it's a follow-on
  deliverable, not a sizing lever.
- The richer scope (three graphs in one phase) is the conscious cost of proving the full network thesis
  in one shot rather than dribbling it across two phases.
