Laundering the proceeds of illicit synthetic opioids

FINTRAC · Operational Alert · January 2025 · selected indicators — paraphrased

Selected ML/TF indicators (paraphrased):

Client receives payroll or deposits inconsistent with their declared occupation and stated income.

Business structures arranged so the ultimate beneficial owner cannot be determined.

Wire or virtual-currency payments to chemical exporters in high-risk source jurisdictions, several sharing the same contact identifiers.

Purchases of pill presses or laboratory equipment with no apparent business need.

Shipping and courier payments paired with chemical-company purchases, out of profile for the client.

Repeated shipments of small parcels under one kilogram, consistent with small-package trafficking.
