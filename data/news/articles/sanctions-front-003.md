An indictment unsealed this week accuses a front company of disguising its ownership to move energy revenue on behalf of a sanctioned producer.

Prosecutors say Caspian Energy Trading presented itself as an independent commodities broker while in fact acting for sanctioned interests. The company's beneficial owner, Reza Daryani, concealed his control behind nominee directors who exercised no real authority over the business, the indictment alleges.

The filing describes payments that were layered through several jurisdictions in quick succession, and an ownership structure deliberately obscured through layers of holding companies. Investigators allege some settlement payments were routed through Aurelia Holdings Ltd, the same Cyprus intermediary named in a separate trade-fraud case.

According to prosecutors, the company repeatedly changed its banking relationships after brief periods of activity, and provided incomplete or inconsistent information about the source of its funds when asked.
