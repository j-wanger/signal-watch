At the center of the alleged scheme is Volkov Maritime Logistics, a freight broker that prosecutors say arranged shipments on paper for buyers it never identified. According to the filing, invoices were systematically overstated by as much as 300 percent, with the excess funds routed to accounts controlled by Aurelia Holdings Ltd, a Cyprus-registered intermediary that prosecutors describe as having no discernible commercial purpose. Shipping documents listed goods inconsistent with the declared value, and the same cargo was re-billed under different descriptions across successive invoices.

Prosecutors also allege the network relied on Eastgate Freight Forwarding to file customs paperwork that misstated the nature of the goods. A logistics associate named Andrei Petrov is quoted in intercepted messages discussing the arrangements.

The indictment notes payments moving rapidly in and out of the intermediary accounts with little time held, and transactions structured to fall just under the amounts that trigger mandatory reporting.
