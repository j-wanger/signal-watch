# Working Knowledge

- [uses: 1] FINTRAC's "suspicious transaction indicators" guidance is EXACTLY 11 per-sector `indicators-indicateurs/<sector>_mltf-eng` pages (financial-entities, MSB, securities, life-insurance, accountants, BC-notaries, DPMS, casinos, real-estate, virtual-currency, crown-agents) — there is NO indicator page for the 2024 sectors (mortgage / armoured-car / cheque-cashing / factoring / financing-leasing). The STR-reporting (`str-dod`) and sanctions-evasion (`sanctions-eng`) guidance pages are PROCESS guidance and enumerate NO indicators (honestly non-derivable; they point back to the 11 sector pages). The remaining derivable FINTRAC indicator frontier lives in the `/intel/` family (operational alerts + Special Bulletins — e.g. OA001 tax-evasion-real-estate, the sanctions-evasion SB, the Russia-linked-ML SB, the dual-use-goods advisory), the SAME family as `data/fintrac/`. Source-completeness ≠ representation-completeness: all 10 derived sector pages can RENDER yet still look incomplete if the lens groups by a doc-level overlay that buckets them. (Phase 37: established while sizing the "only 7 docs under the FINTRAC guidance typology" gap.)
  source: data/fintrac-guidance + fintrac-canafe.canada.ca (project) | activated: 2026-06-08 | tier: 3
- [uses: 1] A FINTRAC per-sector indicator page is INHERENTLY MULTI-TYPOLOGY — it enumerates indicators across bribery/corruption, TF, structuring-below-threshold, wire transfers, non-Canadian jurisdictions, etc. — so a doc→ONE-typology overlay (`data/typology-map.json`) cannot place it cleanly and forces a catch-all (`fintrac-sector-baselines` held 7 of 10). The honest model is typology PER INDICATOR (an overlay keyed on indicator-id, build-time inheriting the doc typology where absent — keeps the derived records byte-frozen), but BEWARE: many individual indicators are themselves CROSS-CUTTING (structuring serves drug-trafficking AND fraud AND TF), so per-indicator-ONE-typology can merely relocate the catch-all to indicator level — MEASURE the cross-cutting fraction (and how much section-heading→typology is deterministic) before committing to single vs multi-label. (Phase 37.)

The enumerated red-flag list is the derivation surface for a detection signal. (Phase 11: this is the surface `extract_red_flags(md)` deterministically parses — Behavioral/Financial section anchors.)

- [uses: 1] FINTRAC strategic-intelligence comes in MULTIPLE product types that all carry an enumerated ML/TF-indicator list (the derivation surface): Operational ALERTS and Operational BRIEFS (e.g. real-estate FINTRAC-2016-OB001). Heading vocabulary is heterogeneous — the OAs head their list with the forward "Money laundering indicators", but the Briefs (and some OAs) use the INVERTED "Indicators of <X>" form (often markitdown-fragmented, e.g. "4. INDICATORS OF MONEY / LAUNDERING IN REAL ESTATE"). A FINTRAC rf_region anchor must cover BOTH forms, connector-gated to EXCLUDE the boilerplate sentence "Indicators of <ML/TF> can be thought of as red flags …" that opens most FINTRAC OAs before their real heading. Also: a 2021 OA may reproduce an older OA's indicators in an APPENDIX — derive the doc's OWN primary list, not the cross-doc reproduction. (Phase 23: `_RF_HEADER_FINTRAC`/`_RF_HEADER_FINTRAC_INV`.)
  source: data/fintrac (project) | activated: 2026-06-06 | tier: 3
- [uses: 1] A derived indicator's `capability` (C1–C28) + `data_source` (D1–D20) codes are LOAD-BEARING, not metadata: they drive every downstream coverage field (status, data, build_rec, build_logic via the cover×data matrix + per-capability templates) AND the Phase-29 capability lens + Phase-30 data-source lens (the demo's executive views). A mis-assigned code silently shows a wrong posture corpus-wide. The grounding gate checks the verbatim `flag` is FAITHFUL; it never checks the C/D tagging is CORRECT — that dimension is unguarded. (Phase 34.)
