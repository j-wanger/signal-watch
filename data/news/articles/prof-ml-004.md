A regulator has accused an accountant of running a professional money-laundering service for a string of cash-intensive businesses, using his firm's client accounts to disguise the origin of the funds.

The accountant, Marcus Bellweather, operated through a practice called Harbour Lane Accountancy, according to the regulator's findings. Among the businesses he served was Greenfield Car Wash Group, a chain whose cash deposits were far larger than its visible trade could support.

The findings describe cash deposits broken into smaller amounts and paid in across multiple branches, and client-account funds commingled so that legitimate and illicit money could not be distinguished. Invoices were inflated to justify transfers that had no underlying service, the regulator says, and funds were moved through the firm's client account in rapid succession to obscure their trail.

The regulator also noted deposits that did not match the customer's stated occupation or income, and a pattern of round-sum payments with no supporting documentation.
