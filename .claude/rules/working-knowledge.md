- [uses: 1] FINTRAC's "suspicious transaction indicators" guidance is EXACTLY 11 per-sector `indicators-indicateurs/<sector>_mltf-eng` pages (financial-entities, MSB, securities, life-insurance, accountants, BC-notaries, DPMS, casinos, real-estate, virtual-currency, crown-agents) — there is NO indicator page for the 2024 sectors (mortgage / armoured-car / cheque-cashing / factoring / financing-leasing). The STR-reporting (`str-dod`) and sanctions-evasion (`sanctions-eng`) guidance pages are PROCESS guidance and enumerate NO indicators (honestly non-derivable; they point back to the 11 sector pages). The remaining derivable FINTRAC indicator frontier lives in the `/intel/` family (operational alerts + Special Bulletins — e.g. OA001 tax-evasion-real-estate, the sanctions-evasion SB, the Russia-linked-ML SB, the dual-use-goods advisory), the SAME family as `data/fintrac/`. Source-completeness ≠ representation-completeness: all 10 derived sector pages can RENDER yet still look incomplete if the lens groups by a doc-level overlay that buckets them.
