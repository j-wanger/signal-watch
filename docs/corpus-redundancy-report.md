# Corpus Redundancy Report — the §13 fm-1 frontier, measured

> **Illustrative data & outputs.** NON-SHIP analysis (the `blueprint-report.html` / `probe-history.md`
> class). Read-only over the committed corpus; the ship corpus (`corpus.html`, `dist/corpus`, every
> `data/*/derived/*.json`, the overlays) is byte-frozen and **untouched**. No de-duplication is
> performed and **no similarity/overlap/lift figure is presented as validated**. Every number carries
> its measurement definition. Generated by `scripts/corpus_redundancy.py` (stdlib, deterministic);
> reproduce with `python3 scripts/corpus_redundancy.py --report` and `--selftest`. Phase 51, 2026-06-16.

## Why this exists

The program's blueprint names **§13 failure-mode-1 (correlated double-counting)**: at library scale,
grounded signals are *"massively redundant across regulators and history (the corpus already refuses
cross-regulator dedup on honesty grounds)… the composition layer must model redundancy explicitly or
refuse to aggregate."* That redundancy was **asserted, never measured**. This report measures its
*exposure* honestly — composition-as-redundancy-management on real grounded data — without computing a
forbidden similarity number on the ship artifact and without de-duplicating the corpus.

The measurement is the measure-first workstream's first deliverable: converting a chosen-not-measured
headline into a measured result.

## What was measured (committed corpus, the corpus's own labels)

**2,251 indicators** across 5 sources / 3 regulators (FINTRAC 1,705 · FinCEN 495 · OFAC 51), each
carrying the corpus's resolved labels: **typology** (per-indicator override from
`indicator-typology-map.json` else doc-level inherit from `typology-map.json`; 350 overrides + 1,901
inherits + **0 unmapped**), **capability** (the C-code on the derived record), **regulator** (the
agency derived from the source — FinCEN advisories + alerts are one regulator).

## T1 — deterministic co-occurrence UPPER BOUND (in-constraint)

**Definition.** An indicator *cross-regulator co-occurs* iff another regulator has an indicator in the
**same (typology, capability) cell**. This is honest union/co-occurrence arithmetic over existing
committed labels — a candidate-redundancy **ceiling**, *not* a claim that two indicators are the same
(a shared label cell is not the same indicator).

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| cross-**agency** co-occurrence upper bound | **0.325** (731 / 2,251 indicators, in 68 / 364 shared cells) |
| cross-**jurisdiction** (US vs Canada) upper bound | 0.288 (649 / 2,251) |

**Top cross-agency cells** (indicator count · typology / capability → regulators): corruption/C17 **118**
(FINTRAC, FinCEN) · corruption/C20 59 · terrorist-financing/C19 40 · corruption/C15 31 ·
terrorist-financing/C18 29 · terrorist-financing/C20 28 · sanctions-evasion/C23 25 (FinCEN, OFAC) ·
corruption/C22 20. The redundancy *exposure* is concentrated in **PEP/corruption and terrorist-
financing**, where FINTRAC's sector guidance and FinCEN's advisories cover the same typologies.

## T2 — sampled semantic-equivalence REFINEMENT (measure-not-claim)

The T1 ceiling overcounts: two indicators in the same cell may describe *different facets*. A
**deterministic sample** of 24 cross-regulator co-occurring pairs (seed 0, of 2,315; reproduce with
`--sample 24 --seed 0`) was judged by a **blind rater** for semantic equivalence — *does the same
underlying behaviour a monitoring rule would target get described?* The judgments are a committed
fixture (`data/corpus-redundancy/equiv-sample.json`), replayed deterministically (the Phase-34
neural-dimension pattern), **single-rater and illustrative**; consensus (≥2 independent raters) is
**deferred-with-owner**. Sample integrity is verified (the committed judgments match the seeded sample).

| Verdict | Count (of 24) |
|---|---|
| equivalent (same behaviour) | **1** |
| partial (related, but a rule for one misses the other) | 15 |
| distinct (different behaviour, shared label only) | 8 |

- **Equivalence rate:** 0.042 strict · 0.354 partial-as-half band (consensus-class, never ground truth).
- **Estimated genuine cross-agency redundancy = upper bound × rate = 0.325 × 0.042 ≈ 1.4%** (band ~11.5%).

## The finding

**Cross-regulator redundancy exposure is real but modest, and the corpus's refusal to de-duplicate is
empirically vindicated.** Up to ~32.5% of indicators share a (typology, capability) cell with another
regulator, but on a blind read only ~1/24 of those pairs describe genuinely the same behaviour — most
(15/24) are *partial* (distinct facets of one typology, e.g. the sham-charity-soliciting side vs the
donor-to-a-flagged-cause side) and a third (8/24) are *distinct* despite the shared label. **De-duping
on the shared label would erase real regulatory distinctions** — which is exactly why the corpus
refuses it. For the composition layer (§13 fm-1), this bounds the double-counting problem: it is
concentrated in PEP/corruption and TF, and genuine duplication is in the low single digits to ~10%,
not a pervasive collapse.

## Honesty boundary (what this is NOT)

- **Not a de-duplication** and not a validated similarity/overlap/lift number on any ship artifact —
  the ship corpus stays byte-frozen and continues to show honest inventory counts only.
- The T1 ceiling is a **label co-occurrence** count, not a semantic claim.
- The T2 rate is a **single-rater illustrative** estimate — its equivalent/partial/distinct line is
  judgmental; **consensus (≥2 raters) and a larger sample are deferred-with-owner**. The point is the
  reproducible machinery + an honest first instance, not a final number.
- Sampling parameters (n=24, seed=0) are **chosen, not derived**.

python3 scripts/corpus_redundancy.py --sample 24 --seed 0   # the exact judged pairs
